Must-dos by country
🇰🇿Kazakhstan
8 must-dos
  • Aral Sea ship graveyard, Moynaq — the most emotionally powerful stop on the entire trip. Rusting Soviet boats stranded in desert. Total silence. You will feel this one for a long time
  • Charyn Canyon — day trip from Almaty. 300m deep, 90km long, carved rock formations that look genuinely alien. The Central Asian Grand Canyon
  • Big Almaty Lake — turquoise alpine lake at 2,500m, 30km from the city. The color is impossible. Combine with Charyn in one driver day
  • Shymbulak gondola — cable car to 2,260m above Almaty for summer wildflower meadows and views over the entire basin
  • Medeu skating rink — walk down from Shymbulak. Site of 126 world speed skating records. Enormous rink in a mountain bowl
  • Green Bazaar, Almaty — final shopping day. Dried apricots, walnuts, spices, and pomegranate to bring home from the trip
  • Try kazy — smoked horse sausage, the distinctive Kazakh dish. An acquired taste absolutely worth acquiring at least once
  • Stand somewhere in the steppe — find a spot with nothing visible in any direction. Kazakhstan's real gift. Nothing quite like the scale of it
🇰🇬Kyrgyzstan
11 must-dos
  • Song Kul Lake — 2 nights at 3,016m with nomad family. Zero signal, zero electricity. The best thing on this trip
  • Tian Shan horse trek — multi-day above 3,200m from Karakol. Arrange independently through your guesthouse
  • Issyk-Kul southern shore — yurt camp stay, horseback along the steppe, hot springs at Ak Bulak
  • Altyn Arashan hot springs — ride or hike up the valley, soak in outdoor thermal pools at 2,800m overnight
  • Eagle hunting, Bokonbaevo — authentic demonstration, few tourists. Only accessible through a driver who knows the family
  • Ala-Archa gorge — day hike from Bishkek to glacier viewpoints at 3,400m. 40km from the city
  • Sulaiman-Too, Osh — UNESCO sacred mountain. Climb for panoramic views over the city and the Fergana Valley
  • Jayma Bazaar, Osh — 2,000-year-old covered market. No tourist section, just real life happening around you
  • Jeti-Oguz — Seven Bulls red rock formations and Valley of Flowers near Karakol
  • Drink kumys — fermented mare's milk with a nomad family at Song Kul or any yurt camp. Non-negotiable
  • Eat beshbarmak — boiled lamb on flat pasta, eaten with hands. The national dish at its most authentic in a homestay
🇹🇯Tajikistan
11 must-dos
  • Pamir Highway (M41) — full 4-day route Osh to Khorog. One of the greatest road trips on earth. Non-negotiable
  • Ak-Baital Pass — 4,655m. The highest point of the journey. Standing here is something you will not forget
  • Karakul Lake — volcanic crater lake at 3,914m with 7,000m peaks behind it. Surreal blue in the brown desert
  • Wakhan Corridor — stand at the Panj River with Afghanistan 30m across the water. Wave to people on the other side
  • Murgab overnight — the most remote town on the route. 3,600m on the plateau. Nothing around for miles
  • Khorog border walk — walk the riverbank with Afghanistan visible across the Panj. Khorog Botanical Garden
  • Alauddin Lakes, Fann Mountains — turquoise glacial lakes at altitude. Each one a different shade. Among the most beautiful things on this trip
  • Iskanderkul Lake — Alexander the Great's lake. A still emerald reservoir at 2,195m. Brief stop or overnight
  • Bibi Fatima hot springs — sacred hot spring in the Wakhan Valley. Stop on the Pamir route
  • Tea with a stranger — someone will invite you in. Accept every time. The hospitality here is the real highlight of Tajikistan
  • Eat qurutob — flatbread soaked in sour liquid qurt with herbs and onion. The national dish. Try it in Dushanbe
🇺🇿Uzbekistan
11 must-dos
  • Registan at night, Samarkand — visit in daylight once, then return at night when lit up. The second visit is the one you remember forever
  • Shah-i-Zinda, Samarkand — narrow street of 14th century blue-tiled mausoleums. Go at dusk with fewer people
  • Cooking class, Samarkand — make plov, samsa, shashlik in a family kitchen. Best €12 spent on the entire trip
  • Bukhara old city after 9pm — when all day tourists have gone. Empty alleys, lantern light, genuinely ancient feeling
  • Lyab-i Hauz, Bukhara — evening tea around the pool with locals and travelers. One of the great travel evenings in Central Asia
  • Kalon Minaret, Bukhara — 12th century tower that Genghis Khan reportedly spared. The only thing he ever spared
  • Khiva at dawn — arrive before any tour groups. The empty walled city in early morning light is the best photography moment of the trip
  • Islam Khoja Minaret, Khiva — climb for 360° views over the entire walled city at sunset
  • Tashkent metro — buy a ticket and ride the full line just to see the Soviet tile murals and chandeliers at each station
  • Plov Centre, Tashkent — arrive at 8am sharp. The national dish at its absolute best. Sells out by noon without exception
  • Siab Bazaar, Samarkand — spices, dried fruits, fresh non bread. The bread is sacred: never place upside down, never drop it

Your comfort limit vs the route
Your max is 28°C. Bishkek, Osh, and Dushanbe exceed that — but you're in mountains for most of August. Cities are short stays (1–2 days). Move early, rest midday.
Uzbekistan in early September is exactly your window — the July 38–42°C has dropped to 24–30°C by the time you arrive
Pamir Highway window: M41 reliably open mid-June to mid-September. You cross it Aug 25–29 — safely within the window
Song Kul and Pamir nights: sub-zero possible even in August. Down jacket and warm sleeping layers are not optional

How to book each stop
🇰🇿 Almaty — Aug 12–14
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — search "hostel Almaty". Target: €15–20/night. No advance booking more than 2–3 weeks ahead needed.
Activities
Charyn Canyon + Big Almaty Lake — do both in one driver day. Don't book in advance. Ask at your hostel to find other guests splitting a car, or use GetYourGuide (~€15–18/person). Total car for group of 4: ~€50–60.
Shymbulak gondola — buy cable car ticket on site. No booking. ~$20 return. Marshrutka #91 or taxi from centre (~1,500 KZT each way).
Green Bazaar — just walk in. No booking ever.
Trusted companies
GetYourGuide Almaty ↗ for day tours. shymbulak.com ↗ for gondola info.
🇰🇬 Bishkek — Aug 14–16
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — "Apple Hostel" or "Nomads Guesthouse" are solid. €8–12/night.
Activities
Ala-Archa gorge — take marshrutka #265 to the end then taxi (~500 KGS each way). Park entry at the gate (~200 KGS). No booking needed.
Get Kyrgyz SIM here — Beeline or Megacom, ~500 KGS. Don't leave without it.
Nothing needs advance booking in Bishkek
🇰🇬 Issyk-Kul — Aug 16–19
Accommodation
Yurt camp — book 3–4 weeks ahead. Fills up in August. Email CBT Kyrgyzstan (cbtkyrgyzstan.kg) or search "yurt camp Issyk-Kul southern shore" on Booking.com. ~2,500 KGS/person/night including meals.
Activities
Horse riding — arrange on arrival at the yurt camp. No advance booking. ~2,500 KGS/horse/day + 3,000 KGS/horseman shared.
Ak Bulak hot springs — shared taxi from Karakol town. No booking.
Trusted companies
CBT Kyrgyzstan ↗ · +996 770 443 021
🇰🇬 Karakol — Aug 19–21
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — "Yak Tours Guesthouse" or "Duet Guesthouse". €10–14/night. Book 1–2 weeks ahead.
Activities
Tian Shan horse trek — arrange at your guesthouse directly. Do NOT book the website package. Ask the owner to call a horseman. ~2,500 KGS/horse/day + 3,500 KGS/guide/day shared across 4. Budget ~$200/person for 4–5 days.
Altyn Arashan — same: arrange through guesthouse. Horse up the valley or hike. ~2,500 KGS/horse + horseman cost shared.
Jeti-Oguz rocks — marshrutka from town, no booking.
Key note
CBT Karakol local office can also help but charges more than going direct through the guesthouse owner. Ask the guesthouse first.
🇰🇬 Song Kul Lake — Aug 21–23
Getting there — book transport ahead
4x4 from Kochkor — arrange at any Kochkor guesthouse on arrival there. "Minbashi Guesthouse" is the main hub. ~3,000–5,000 KGS for the whole vehicle one way, split 4 = ~750–1,250 KGS/person. They also help find the return transport.
Yurt camp — book ahead, this one matters
Book 3–4 weeks before arrival. Contact CBT Kochkor directly: +996 700 570 202. Or email cbtkyrgyzstan.kg. Specify arrival date and number of people. ~2,500 KGS/person/night with dinner + breakfast included.
You can also walk up and book on arrival if you arrive early enough, but August fills out. Don't risk it.
Activities
Horse day rides at the lake — arrange with the yurt camp host directly on arrival. ~2,500 KGS/horse + horseman cost shared. No advance booking.
Cost summary
4x4 Kochkor→Song Kul: ~1,000 KGS/person · Yurt 2 nights: 5,000 KGS/person · Horse day ride: ~1,500 KGS/person → total ~7,500 KGS (~$83/person)
🇰🇬 Osh — Aug 23–25 · Pamir jeep hub
Accommodation
"Osh Guesthouse" is the main traveler hub. Book on Booking.com or direct. €8–10/night. The owner has Pamir jeep contacts — this is the right place to stay.
Pamir Highway jeep — most important booking on the trip
Option A — arrange in Osh (recommended): Ask your guesthouse owner to find a jeep for your group. As a group of 4 you fill a Land Cruiser perfectly which puts you in a strong position. Cost: ~$0.70–0.90/km, Osh→Khorog (~730km) = ~$510–660 total = ~$130–165/person. Agree the full price and route in advance.
Option B — book ahead online: Use Meta Travel (meta-travel.com) or PECTA Pamir (pectapamir.com). More reliable, slightly more expensive. Good if you want the trip confirmed before you leave Iceland.
Option C — find partners before the trip: Post on the "Pamir Highway Hitchhikers" Facebook group and on Caravanistan Forum to split a jeep with other travelers if you want a 5th or 6th person to lower per-person cost further.
Trusted companies
meta-travel.com ↗ — established Pamir operator · pectapamir.com ↗ — community-based, Khorog end
🇹🇯 Pamir Highway — Aug 25–29
Homestays en route
Pay on arrival — no advance booking. Every village along the M41 has homestay options. ~$12–15/person/night including dinner and breakfast. Your driver knows where to stop.
Murghab has a few basic guesthouses. Ask your driver to call ahead from Karakul. No online booking exists.
Cash warning
Bring all cash from Osh. No ATMs after Osh until Khorog. Budget minimum $200/person in USD or local currency for the full 4-day crossing including accommodation, food, and unexpected costs.
🇹🇯 Khorog — Aug 29–30
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com or through PECTA. "Serena Aga Khan" is the nicest option (~€35/room). Budget guesthouses: €10–15/person. One night — no need to book more than a week ahead.
Activities
PECTA office, Khorog City Park — walk in. Arrange Wakhan guide here if you want one. visitpamirs.com ↗
Afghan Saturday market — free, just show up on the island in the Panj River. Make sure your arrival timing lines up.
🇹🇯 Dushanbe — Aug 30–31
Accommodation
Rumi Hotel (recommended, comfortable) or Green House Hostel (cheaper, good kitchen). Book on Booking.com 1–2 weeks ahead. €15–35/night.
Transport to Fann Mountains
Shared taxi from Dushanbe toward Penjikent — departs from the taxi stand near Green Bazaar, mornings. ~40 TJS/person. No booking — just show up early.
🇹🇯 Fann Mountains — Sep 1–4
Accommodation
Book through Advantour ↗ or arrange in Penjikent on arrival. Basic mountain guesthouses and camp. ~€12–15/person/night with meals.
Guide
Hire a local guide in Penjikent — ask at your guesthouse. ~€20–25/day. Worth it for the Alauddin Lakes trek. They also arrange a donkey for luggage (~€5–8/day).
Border crossing to Uzbekistan
Penjikent → Samarkand: shared taxi to the border, walk across, shared taxi on the Uzbek side. ~$5–10 total. Easy crossing, usually under 1 hour.
🇺🇿 Samarkand — Sep 4–7
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — find a guesthouse inside the old city. "Antica B&B" or "Emir" are solid. €12–18/night. Book 2–3 weeks ahead — September is busy.
Activities
Registan, Shah-i-Zinda, Gur-e-Amir — buy tickets on site. No advance booking. ~€5–8 entry each.
Cooking class — book on GetYourGuide ↗ or ask your guesthouse to recommend a family. ~€12/person.
Transport to Bukhara
Afrosiyob fast train — book on uzrailpass.uz ↗. ~$5–10/person. Book at least 2 weeks ahead in September.
🇺🇿 Bukhara — Sep 7–10
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — guesthouse inside the old city walls. "Malika Bukhara" or "Fatima Guesthouse". €12–18/night. Book 2–3 weeks ahead.
Activities
Ark Fortress, Kalon Minaret, Lyab-i Hauz — all walkable, pay entry on site. No booking.
Walking tour — book on GetYourGuide ↗. ~€18/person.
Transport to Khiva
Shared taxi Bukhara→Urgench (~5h, ~$8/person) then taxi Urgench→Khiva (30min, ~$3). Or arrange a private driver for ~$40 total for all 4.
🇺🇿 Khiva — Sep 10–12
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — stay inside Itchan Kala (the walled city). "Malika Khiva" or "Orient Star". €15–22/night. Book 2–3 weeks ahead — limited options inside the walls.
Activities
Itchan Kala entry ticket covers most monuments. ~€5. Buy at the main gate. No booking.
Dawn photography walk with guide — book on GetYourGuide ↗. ~€10/person. Worth it.
Transport to Nukus (Aral Sea)
Taxi Khiva→Nukus ~200km, ~2.5h. ~$20–25 for the whole car. Arrange through your guesthouse the evening before.
🇰🇿 Aral Sea / Moynaq — Sep 12–14
Getting there
Taxi Khiva→Nukus (~200km, ~2.5h, ~$20–25 whole car). From Nukus, hire a 4x4 to Moynaq (~130km south).
4x4 to Moynaq + ship graveyard
Option A — Stantours: Book through stantours.com ↗. Reliable, organized, includes 4x4 + guide. ~€40–50/person for the group of 4.
Option B — arrange in Nukus: Any guesthouse in Nukus can organize a driver and 4x4 to Moynaq. ~$60–80 for the whole vehicle, split 4 = ~$15–20/person.
Accommodation
Moynaq has very basic guesthouses (~€8–10/person). Or stay in Nukus (better options, ~€15–20/person) and day-trip to Moynaq.
Nukus → Tashkent flight
Book on uzbekistan-airways.com ↗ or through a local travel agent. ~$30–50/person. Book 2–3 weeks ahead. Flight is ~1.5h.
🇺🇿 Tashkent — Sep 14–15 · fly home
Accommodation
Book on Booking.com — budget hotel near the metro. One night. €15–25/night. No advance booking more than a week ahead needed.
Activities
Plov Centre — arrive at 8am sharp, no booking, cash only. Sells out by noon. ~$3–5/person for a plate.
Metro — buy a card at the station (~5,000 UZS), ride the full line. No booking.
Exit flight
TAS→IST→KEF — already booked. Airport: Tashkent International. Use Yandex Go for the taxi to airport (~15,000–20,000 UZS). Or walk LEFT past the parking lot for returning drivers at ~25,000 UZS max.

Horse Riding in Kyrgyzstan — Everything You Need to Know
  • Only stallions are used for riding — mares are used for kumys and breeding. You will see the mares and foals free in the pastures
  • Say "chu" to go and "trrrrrr" or "dak dak" to stop. Learn these before you get on
  • Never wear a rain poncho on horseback — scares the horses. Use a waterproof jacket only
  • Don't let horses eat along the trail or they'll decide they're the boss
  • Check horse health before agreeing to any trip near Ala Kul — exploitation is documented there
  • Border permits for Kel Suu and Chatyr Kul — apply via CBT Naryn email, 2 weeks ahead, 1,000–2,000 KGS
  • Book ahead for July–August — horses book out in peak season

Tajikistan — Practical Logistics Guide
  • GBAO permit at OVIR Dushanbe: only 20 Somoni (~$2) if you go in person. Also in Khujand and Panjakent
  • Best time: September–October — hiking still open, cooler temps, fresh fruit everywhere, fewer tourists than summer
  • Homestays on Pamir Highway: ~$12-13/night including dinner + breakfast. Dushanbe dorm: ~$4-5. Meals: ~$2
  • Drones are allowed in Tajikistan for recreational use — no permit needed
  • Your Persian covers Tajikistan completely. Tajik is a dialect of Farsi. In rural Fann Mountains, some families barely speak Russian. Your language is the biggest advantage you have
